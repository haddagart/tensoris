# Honorable Mentions & Recommended Tools

We gratefully acknowledge the open-source libraries, scientific toolkits, and MLOps platforms that empower modern Deep Learning research and Computer Vision workflows. Below are our recommended tools for building robust, scalable AI projects.

---

## 🧠 Core Frameworks & Math Foundations

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **PyTorch**
    ---
    The foundational deep learning framework used to build, train, and scale state-of-the-art vision and multimodal models with dynamic GPU acceleration.

    [![PyTorch](https://img.shields.io/badge/PyTorch-2.0%2B-EE4C2C?style=flat-square&logo=pytorch&logoColor=white)](https://pytorch.org/)

-   **NumPy**
    ---
    The core package for fast matrix and tensor mathematical computations underlying all image arrays and model data.

    [![NumPy](https://img.shields.io/badge/NumPy-Math-013243?style=flat-square&logo=numpy&logoColor=white)](https://numpy.org/)

-   **SciPy**
    ---
    The definitive scientific computing toolkit for advanced statistical tests, spatial calculations, and scientific metric validations.

    [![SciPy](https://img.shields.io/badge/SciPy-Science-8CA0D7?style=flat-square&logo=scipy&logoColor=white)](https://scipy.org/)

</div>

## 👁️ Computer Vision & Data Augmentation

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **OpenCV**
    ---
    The standard computer vision library for loading, transforming, and streaming real-time image and video frames.

    [![OpenCV](https://img.shields.io/badge/OpenCV-Vision-5C3EE8?style=flat-square&logo=opencv&logoColor=white)](https://opencv.org/)

-   **Albumentations**
    ---
    A fast, high-performance data augmentation library used to apply complex geometric, pixel-level, and weather-simulation transformations to boost model robustness.

    [![Albumentations](https://img.shields.io/badge/Albumentations-Augmentation-FF6F00?style=flat-square)](https://albumentations.ai/)

-   **Hugging Face Transformers**
    ---
    Essential for testing multi-modal vision-language research, Vision Transformers (ViTs), and DETR object detection models.

    [![Hugging Face](https://img.shields.io/badge/%F0%9F%A4%97%20Hugging%20Face-Transformers-FFD21E?style=flat-square)](https://huggingface.co/)

-   **MMDetection**
    ---
    OpenMMLab's modular computer vision toolbox widely used in academia to compare YOLO against Transformer-based or anchor-free detectors.

    [![MMDetection](https://img.shields.io/badge/OpenMMLab-MMDetection-1296DB?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmdetection)

-   **Torch-CAM**
    ---
    A Class Activation Mapping (CAM) library essential for computer vision research to explain model decision-making via visual heatmaps.

    [![Torch-CAM](https://img.shields.io/badge/Interpretability-Torch--CAM-E040FB?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/frgousseau/torch-cam)

</div>

## 🎬 Video Understanding & Stream Processing

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **PyTorchVideo**
    ---
    Meta AI's specialized research toolkit providing dataset loaders, models, and transforms optimized for video action recognition.

    [![PyTorchVideo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Meta%20AI-PyTorchVideo-0467DF?style=flat-square)](https://pytorchvideo.org/)

-   **MMAction2**
    ---
    OpenMMLab's open-source codebase dedicated to video understanding, action recognition, and spatio-temporal action localization.

    [![MMAction2](https://img.shields.io/badge/OpenMMLab-MMAction2-00BCD4?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmaction2)

-   **Decord**
    ---
    An efficient video slicing library providing fast, random, and frame-accurate access to video data via hardware-accelerated video decoding.

    [![Decord](https://img.shields.io/badge/Video%20Decoding-Decord-4CAF50?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/dmlc/decord)

-   **vLLM**
    ---
    High-throughput inference engine essential when bridging video analysis with Large Vision-Language Models (Video-LLMs) for video captioning and QA.

    [![vLLM](https://img.shields.io/badge/Inference-vLLM-7C4DFF?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm)

-   **Diffusers**
    ---
    Hugging Face's library for generative video analysis, frame prediction, and synthetic video data generation.

    [![Diffusers](https://img.shields.io/badge/Generative-Diffusers-FF5722?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers)

</div>

## 🎯 Multi-Object Tracking & Analytics

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Roboflow Supervision**
    ---
    A model-agnostic utility framework providing video processing tools, line-crossing counters, zone analytics, and visual overlays for papers.

    [![Supervision](https://img.shields.io/badge/Roboflow-Supervision-8C52FF?style=flat-square)](https://supervision.roboflow.com/)

-   **ByteTrack**
    ---
    A state-of-the-art tracking algorithm that associates almost all detection boxes instead of only high-score ones to maintain trajectory continuity.

    [![ByteTrack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tracking-ByteTrack-009688?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/ifzhang/ByteTrack)

-   **BoT-SORT**
    ---
    A top-tier tracker combining motion and appearance information, ideal for tracking objects through long occlusions and camera motion.

    [![BoT-SORT](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tracking-BoT--SORT-3F51B5?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/NirAharon/BoT-SORT)

-   **DeepOCSORT**
    ---
    An advanced tracking framework built for robustness against camera jitter, nonlinear motion, and objects that temporarily stop moving.

    [![DeepOCSORT](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tracking-DeepOCSORT-FF9800?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/GlintTTS/DeepOCSORT)

-   **TrackEval**
    ---
    The definitive academic evaluation suite used to compute standardized multi-object tracking metrics (MOTA, IDF1, HOTA) for research papers.

    [![TrackEval](https://img.shields.io/badge/Evaluation-TrackEval-E91E63?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/JonathonL收藏/TrackEval)

</div>

## 📊 MLOps, Experiment Tracking & Data Quality

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Weights & Biases (W&B)**
    ---
    The industry standard MLOps platform for tracking loss curves, running hyperparameter sweeps, and managing model research artifacts.

    [![W&B](https://img.shields.io/badge/MLOps-Weights%20%26%20Biases-FFBE00?style=flat-square&logo=weightsandbiases&logoColor=black)](https://wandb.ai/)

-   **ClearML**
    ---
    A robust open-source MLOps platform providing complete version control for datasets, code, and model weights in a unified dashboard.

    [![ClearML](https://img.shields.io/badge/MLOps-ClearML-00D2FF?style=flat-square)](https://clear.ml/)

-   **FiftyOne**
    ---
    Voxel51's open-source dataset visualization tool for curating training data, analyzing model false positives, and exposing dataset bias.

    [![FiftyOne](https://img.shields.io/badge/Visualization-FiftyOne-FF3366?style=flat-square)](https://voxel51.com/fiftyone/)

-   **Cleanlab**
    ---
    An algorithmic data quality framework that automatically detects data errors, label noise, and dataset outliers.

    [![Cleanlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/Data%20Quality-Cleanlab-22C55E?style=flat-square)](https://cleanlab.ai/)

</div>

## ⚡ Deployment, Acceleration & Interactive Demos

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **ONNX & ONNX Runtime**
    ---
    An open format and runtime engine for exporting PyTorch models and executing them efficiently across diverse hardware platforms.

    [![ONNX](https://img.shields.io/badge/Export-ONNX-005CED?style=flat-square&logo=onnx&logoColor=white)](https://onnx.ai/)

-   **NVIDIA TensorRT**
    ---
    An SDK for high-performance deep learning inference that optimizes trained networks for fast execution on NVIDIA GPUs.

    [![TensorRT](https://img.shields.io/badge/NVIDIA-TensorRT-76B900?style=flat-square&logo=nvidia&logoColor=white)](https://developer.nvidia.com/tensorrt)

-   **Gradio**
    ---
    A lightweight Python library for building fast, interactive web applications to demo object detection or segmentation models.

    [![Gradio](https://img.shields.io/badge/UI-Gradio-FF7C00?style=flat-square)](https://gradio.app/)

-   **uv (Astral)**
    ---
    An extremely fast Python package manager written in Rust, used throughout this template for high-speed dependency resolution.

    [![uv](https://img.shields.io/badge/Package%20Manager-uv-261230?style=flat-square)](https://astral.sh/uv)

-   **ProperDocs & MaterialX**
    ---
    Next-generation static documentation generator and Material theme fork powering this project's documentation.

    [![MaterialX](https://img.shields.io/badge/Theme-MaterialX-4F46E5?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/jaywhj/mkdocs-materialx)

</div>
